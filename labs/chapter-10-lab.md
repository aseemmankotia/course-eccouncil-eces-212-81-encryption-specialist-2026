# Chapter 10 Lab: Cryptanalysis: Breaking Cryptosystems

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Use CrypTool to run a Kasiski examination against a Vigenere-encrypted sample to recover the key length, then use hashcat to crack a small sample of unsalted MD5 password hashes with a wordlist

## Key takeaway

Match the cryptanalysis technique to the cipher structure — monoalphabetic gets frequency analysis, polyalphabetic gets Kasiski, and unsalted hash databases fall to rainbow tables.
