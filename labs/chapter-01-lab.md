# Chapter 1 Lab: Cryptography Foundations and Classical Ciphers

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Use CrypTool 2 to encrypt/decrypt text with Caesar, Vigenere, and Playfair ciphers, then brute-force a Caesar-encrypted message

## Key takeaway

Every classical cipher on the exam is either substitution or transposition — identify which, and you can identify its weakness.
