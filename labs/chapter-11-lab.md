# Chapter 11 Lab: Quantum Computing Threats and Post-Quantum Cryptography

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Use the Open Quantum Safe (liboqs) demo or a NIST PQC reference implementation to generate an ML-KEM (Kyber) key pair and perform an encapsulation/decapsulation exchange

## Key takeaway

Quantum threats hit asymmetric crypto hardest via Shor's algorithm — symmetric crypto only needs longer keys against Grover's algorithm, and NIST has already standardized ML-KEM and ML-DSA as replacements.
