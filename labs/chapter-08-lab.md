# Chapter 8 Lab: SSL/TLS Handshakes and Authentication Protocols

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Capture a live TLS 1.3 handshake to a public HTTPS site using Wireshark, filter on tls.handshake, and identify the ClientHello, ServerHello, and negotiated cipher suite fields

## Key takeaway

Every authentication protocol on the exam differs in whether credentials are exposed in transit — PAP never protects them, CHAP and Kerberos do.
